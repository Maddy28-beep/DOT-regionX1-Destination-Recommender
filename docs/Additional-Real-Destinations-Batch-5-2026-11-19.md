## Duplicate and geography checks

- Compared names and principal aliases with **230 destination and 230 accommodation rows**, including archived entries returned by the model. No exact normalized candidate/alias matches. Public listing names and locations were exported for this check; no account information was read.
- Tagbaobo Falls / Mangongawong Falls excluded: overlaps existing Magongawon Falls (destination 57).
- Suaon Nature Park excluded: the promoted cave areas overlap existing Alina and Kapalong cave-system listings.
- Pag-Asa Farms excluded: existing JKM Mini Zoo (destination 17) is at Pag-Asa Farms, Kapalong.
- Freedom Park is the separately named public open space behind/around the old Tagum city hall / current historical-cultural center, distinct from the new Apokon city hall and Rotary/Tagumpay Park. Provincial Ordinance 870 (2019), approved by Resolution 691, specifically declares the area behind the historical-cultural center as Freedom Park. Do not create another duplicate cultural-center building record. Its selected photograph is historical.
- Roxas Greenway Park is the riverside greenway, separate from Roxas Night Market; do not reuse the night-market record or claim its hours.
- City Hall of Davao is a heritage-building exterior, separate from the nearby cathedral and Osmeña Park. No interior access assumed.
- Almendras Falls here is the Manuel Guianga / Tugbok, Davao City site identified by the geopark and location-specific hiking footage, not the same-name falls in Bamban, Tarlac. Precise entrance and permission still require local confirmation.
- Casilac Falls, Ladyungan Cave and several other leads were not counted: no sufficiently resolved photo/identity/location package was selected for them.

Current fees, hours, coordinates, trail permissions and safe river conditions were not invented. New records retain itinerary_role=excluded pending verified operational details, preserving generated plans. Existing detail-template fallback wording is unchanged.
