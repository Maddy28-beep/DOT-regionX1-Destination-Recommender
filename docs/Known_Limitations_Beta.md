# ExploreDVO beta: known limitations (plain language)

For the professor's trial, 9 November 2026. Build: tag `beta-2026-11-09`.

Updated 19 November 2026 for the version live at that date (commit c4a4a96): items 17 to 26 were added after the first list.

1. No booking. The system plans; it does not reserve or pay for anything.
2. No real-time traffic. Drive times are estimates from distance, not live conditions.
3. A trip cannot be edited by hand: no adding or reordering stops. Change the preferences and generate again (a stop can be swapped for a similar place).
4. Companion type (solo, couple, family, group) is recorded but does not change the plan yet.
5. Interest fit is relative within the places left after filtering, not an absolute score.
6. Ratings and popularity have no effect until a place has enough reviews.
7. Association rules ("people who visit X also visit Y") come from simulated data, not real visitors.
8. Partner portal actions are not written to the audit log yet (admin actions are).
9. Some days have a single far-away stop. Across 460 test trips (two seeds) 27 days came out over 8 hours of driving: 15 on Close or Commutable trips and 12 on Remote trips, where long drives are expected. Each shows a visible warning: "About N hours of driving there and back for this one stop, more than a day should hold, so it has a day of its own. Consider staying closer, or spending a night nearby."
10. When a Close or Commutable trip has to widen its range, places that are too far for one day are left out and the page says so: "Some distant places were left out because they are too far for a single day. Choose 'Remote' to include them." On the live site this notice is rare (19 long or sparse-start test trips never triggered it); it is covered by automated tests.
11. Chatbot: asking about a closed place gives a closure notice and no card. It never invents a price or opening hours that are not in the record.
12. Stays: a stay more than 60 km from every stop of the trip is passed over, except on Remote trips.
13. Sample (placeholder) places are labelled "Sample data, not DOT-verified".
14. Package adoption ("Plan with this Package") needs a published day-by-day schedule. At deployment there were 5 live packages and 0 had schedule rows, so every package page offered "Plan My Trip" instead. For the trial two sample packages (Samal Island Hopping Day Tour and Davao City Cultural Heritage Tour) carry one illustrative sample day each, marked "[SAMPLE]"; the other 3 still have none.
15. Test setting to remove after the trial: `ACCREDITATION_TEST_GRACE_UNTIL=2026-12-31` is set on production, so listings whose accreditation expired stay visible and plannable until 31 December 2026. With the setting removed, an expired listing disappears from the public pages (checked on a restored copy). This is deliberate for the trial and must be removed afterwards.
16. People's Park is marked temporarily closed in the data (until the admin reopens it). While it is closed it is left out of trips and the chatbot says so.
17. A typed pick-up place that cannot be found at all is accepted and the trip is planned from Davao City centre; the header still shows the typed text. The address lookup is limited to the Philippines, so a place with no match at all (nonsense text, or the lookup service being down) has no coordinates for the Davao Region check. A place that is found but is outside Davao Region (for example "Manila Hotel") is refused. Decided on 14 November not to change this before the beta, so a lookup outage never blocks anyone from planning. To revisit after the beta: refuse only when the lookup says "no match", and keep accepting when the lookup itself failed.
18. The suggestions shown while typing a pick-up place list Davao Region places only (since 19 November). Typing a place by hand still works from anywhere, so a visitor from abroad can plan a trip before arriving.
19. Visitors from outside Davao Region (another country, or a Philippine province outside the region): only the "Use my current location" button is switched off, with the message "Your current location is outside Davao Region. Pick-up location selection is not available for your location. If you wish to continue, please indicate your preferred pick-up location." Typing a pick-up place still works and must be inside Davao Region. The airport default is cleared for these visitors and comes back if they change the origin back to Davao Region.
20. Where the visitor travels from now comes first in the form, and everyone answers it, local visitors included. "Prefer not to say" no longer exists, the Philippines needs a province, and the notice no longer begins with "Optional.". The changed notice and Privacy Page wording ("Where you travel from:") still need the professor or data-protection review.
21. Visitor origin statistics are permanent and anonymous: the day, country, province (Philippines), city and a number, with nothing that identifies a person or trip. Counted once per browser session, so the same person on two browsers counts twice. The day is the Philippine day the visitor first planned. No backfill: answers before the 14 November change are not in the permanent counts. City is free text, cleaned (letters, spaces, hyphens, periods, apostrophes; at most 60 characters; Title Case), so "Davao" and "Davao City" are different groups. A group under 5 is never shown on its own, and the CSV gives totals over the chosen range only.
22. Test answers made during QA stay in the permanent visitor-origin counts until an admin removes them. The table was cleared on 19 November (8 test rows, backed up then dropped). It must be cleared again just before the real beta opens if more testing is done.
23. The visitor-origin tables show "Not enough answers yet" until at least 5 visitors have answered, and each group needs 5 on its own to appear.
24. Recommendations are counted once a night, not live. "People who visit A also visit B" and the planner's popularity weight are read from a table rebuilt at 02:30 UTC (10:30 AM Philippine time) by `php artisan association-rules:build`. A new exit-survey answer, import or demo batch changes the suggestions after the next build; the admin Association Rules page says when it was last built. After a big import, run `sudo -u www-data php artisan association-rules:build` on the server. To turn it off (counts live again at once, no deploy): `php artisan features:set association_rules_table off`. The two sets of numbers were compared 3,029 times on live data with no difference.
25. Almost all the data behind the recommendations is demo: on 18 November the 1,002 recorded trips were 997 demo and 5 real, so "real surveys only" has no rules yet. The suggestions demonstrate the method; they are not a finding about real visitors (this adds to item 7). Tested size on simulated data and one PC, not real traffic: recommendations and the planner stay flat up to 500,000 recorded trips. Similar-place swaps depend on the number of places: about 0.05 s per click with today's 151 places, 0.3 s at 500, 1.4 s at 1,500, and about 9 s with too much memory at 5,000. Many people using the site at the same moment was not measured.
26. Logo: the eagle, sun, Mount Apo and durian logo is live in the header, mobile menu, footer, sign-in pages and browser icons. The lettering uses the Barabara typeface, whose licence for use inside a logo has not been checked, and DOT has not approved the logo.
