Research only. No database rows, listing views, accreditation records or planner code were changed.

## Result

The audit identified 99 active real-named destinations missing coordinates. Five research passes found 80 coordinate references: 36, 15, 9, 18 and 2 respectively. The remaining 19 do not yet have a selected coordinate. These are sourced research leads, not verified entrances or fully verified listings.

Reference types: 37 venue area; 1 operator map marker; 1 photo location; 13 natural feature; 6 island area; 1 boat stop; 3 marine map pin; 1 landmark; 2 study area; 8 summit; 1 mapped main entrance; 1 secondary pin; 2 viewpoint; 1 mapped cave entrance; 1 approximate area; 1 identity review. None is independently field-verified as a vehicle entrance.

Hours and fees need separate verification. No current admission fee was approved for import. The climbing-guide fee found distinguishes day registration from camping; those must not be merged into a price range. The later Dayang fee evidence is documented separately in the follow-up.
