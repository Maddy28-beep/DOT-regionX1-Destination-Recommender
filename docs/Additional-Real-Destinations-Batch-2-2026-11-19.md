# More real destinations: second research batch

Research date: 19 November 2026. These 20 candidates were checked against active local destination names and likely aliases. Eighteen have now been applied locally as recorded below; the two caves remain pending accurate photo verification.

Existence is supported by the sources below, but present-day visitor access is not established for every place. DOT accreditation is **not confirmed** for all 20. A DOT directory entry is not an accreditation certificate. Unknown coordinates, rates, opening hours and visit durations must remain blank until verified. Photo-bearing sources are leads, not permission to publish their images.

## Duplicate and boundary exclusions

- Sanipaan Marine Park overlaps the existing Vanishing Island entry; not counted again.
- Panabo Mangrove Park was included in the previous batch; not counted again.
- Babu Santa is held pending comparison with the existing Dayang Beach location and aliases.
- Matigol/Bani Falls was not included because the Arakan/Buda location does not establish a destination inside Davao Region.
- Do not replace a fictional sample name while retaining its invented coordinates, fees or opening hours.

## Suggested next batch

Start with the DOT/LGU directory candidates and secure one accurately matched real photo for each. Photo selection and publication rights remain a separate task. Keep the current image information icon and unconfirmed accreditation treatment. Do not enable trail, cave, private-property or appointment-only destinations in generated itineraries until the required visit details are verified.

## Local application and remaining photo gap

The user authorized all 20 candidates on 19 November 2026. Eighteen were added with one source-matched real photograph each using `destinations:import-more-real-local --apply`. Dats Cave and Asbang Faksol Cave were held: the accessible news photographs describe several Matanao caves collectively, and an image of Su'bon must not be relabeled as either of these caves. The official provincial tourism brochure could not be retrieved from its published URL. A field-study paper confirmed both names but supplied charts rather than usable site photographs. No generic cave photograph was substituted. These two listings have not been created.

The photo files, original URLs, credits, source references and SHA-256 hashes are in `database/data/listing-photos/real-destinations-batch2-2026-11-19/manifest.json`. Photos are recorded for local testing only; public publication rights are not asserted. Visible source marks are preserved. The coastal-road event graphic was rejected and replaced with the city government's real photograph of cyclists at the coastal leisure area.

The importer checks normalized names, slugs and known aliases, including White Peak/Mount Tagubud and Aguacan/Aguakan. It preserves existing records and refuses production execution. No accreditation records are created. The existing legacy public-eligibility flag is separate from accreditation provenance; the existing UI correctly renders the unconfirmed information icon and no DOT badge. All 18 additions use the existing excluded itinerary role while visit details remain unverified. Fees, hours, coordinates and durations were not invented. No shared listing, filtering, sharing, approval or planner implementation was edited.

Validation: 11 focused import and filter tests passed with 443 assertions. All 18 detail responses returned HTTP 200 and their public photo paths exist.
