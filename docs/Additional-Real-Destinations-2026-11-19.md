## Accreditation and publication

Existence as a destination and DOT accreditation are separate checks. A DOT tourism-directory entry confirms that a place is described in that directory; it does not establish an accreditation number. Accreditation is **not confirmed** for every candidate below. Do not mark any of them DOT-accredited without an official record. Unknown prices, hours, coordinates, access restrictions, and photo permissions still need verification before import; do not copy the invented attributes from a sample listing.

## Suggested catalogue treatment

Use the actual destination name and location, a factual source-backed description, one correctly matched real photo with its credit and recorded usage basis, and an accreditation label such as “DOT accreditation not confirmed.” Keep unavailable fields blank. Verification of a place's existence must not automatically enable hiking trails, seasonal attractions, or appointment-only facilities in the itinerary planner.

The current app's `is_accredited` flag also controls public listing eligibility, while accreditation provenance is derived from accreditation records. Any future import must preserve this distinction and must not fabricate an accreditation record or change planner/filter logic merely to make a new place visible.
