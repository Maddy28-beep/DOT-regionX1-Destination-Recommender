The public question uses neutral wording: “Where did you stay?” with “Other / not in the list”. It does not describe the internal accreditation follow-up purpose. Choosing Other opens two fields: accommodation/property name and city/municipality or area. Airbnb and other rentals can be entered normally. Friends/family and no overnight stay are separate choices; no host identity or private home address is requested.

The question is optional. Only overall rating and recommendation remain required unless the visitor chooses to report a manual stay, in which case its name and area must both be supplied. Visitors can add up to five stays; duplicate identical answers within one survey are collapsed. Without JavaScript the first stay and manual fields remain usable, with conditional validation on the server. Existing answers survive validation. Directory options exclude placeholder/sample listings; unlisted places are not silently matched by name.

`exit_survey_stays` stores actual reported stays independently from itinerary suggestions and generic places visited. It links to its parent survey with cascade deletion, and optionally to an accommodation with set-null deletion. Directory names/areas and match status are snapshotted. Historical surveys are not backfilled. No new public listing, QR review, accreditation decision, or Apriori transaction is created just because somebody names a stay.

DOT sees manual entries first, marked **Unverified · manual entry**. These are leads to check, not confirmed unaccredited businesses. A property absent from this application's directory could already be accredited under another name. Directory matches also require checking current accreditation. The report offers date/status filters, counts by property and area, response counts and last reported date, plus aggregate CSV export. Different cities remain separate. Only case and whitespace variants are grouped; spelling variations require human review. Counts are survey responses, not unique people. Friends/family and day trips appear only as aggregate counts, and omissions are shown.

Only `real` survey responses feed the report. Demo, test and itinerary-import records are excluded. Authentication protects both report and export; CSV strings starting with formula markers are escaped. Output uses Blade escaping. Manual data are length limited, status/IDs are derived server-side, invalid/sample IDs are rejected, and day-trip answers cannot be combined with overnight stays. No outreach messages or automatic enforcement actions are sent.
