- 16 existing records: 24 additive amenity entries and 11 field values.

## Evidence and limits

Government service guides, exact museum directory entries, regional tourism profiles and authored firsthand accounts were reviewed. PDF table pages were rendered and inspected. Older and independent facility reports use visible “(reported)” labels and require current availability confirmation. Reported means historically documented, not operator-confirmed today.

Kaputian park service tables include proposed fee headings. Only described services were recorded, with confirmation caveats; no proposed fees, hostel room-only facilities or application processing times were imported as park fees, amenities or visit duration. City Hall and Freedom Park event use requires approval. Private Island Buenavista facilities are identified as reserved-guest facilities. Natural trekking routes do not imply paved or maintained trails.

Museum directory hours conflicting with the official National Museum portal were not used. Generic wheelchair accessibility was not converted into specific ramp or accessible-toilet claims. The museum alias was explicitly matched to the same People’s Park venue; no alias was renamed, merged or published.
